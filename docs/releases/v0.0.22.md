versionCode: 22

## What's new
- Buy ×10 and ×100 now always buy that many, and MAX buys everything you can afford (they used to stop at milestones). NEXT still stops at the next goal and shows where it lands (→ 75)
- Round milestones: 25 (2× speed), 50 (2× speed), 75 (2× pay), 100 (2× speed), 150 (2× pay), 200 (3× pay). Speed-upgrade and mastery counts are in steps of 25 too (Timber Camp mastery is now 75, not 72)
- Each business shows its next milestone and how many to go, with a small progress bar; reaching one flashes the row and says what you got
- A grey buy button says how much more cash you need
- New: Investor milestones on the BOARD tab. Unlocks you keep forever at 10, 50, 250, 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000 investors: a head start of Timber Camps, material managers that stay hired, 36 and 48 hours of time away, upgrades that buy themselves, and starting runs with your early businesses
- The later eras (Microprocessors to Quantum) take longer: Quantum at about 6 hours of nonstop play instead of under 3. The first investor now comes at $100B earned in a run (was $1T), so prestige still lands around Quantum
- Saves battery: the game updates 4 times a second instead of 10, progress bars draw at up to 30 frames a second, and bars off screen stop drawing
- Prestige guide: the Investors panel shows your income now and after a prestige, and the button glows once a prestige would at least double your investors. The prestige prompt shows the same numbers
- A screen after your first prestige shows what you gained, what you kept and what started over, plus your next investor milestone
- Challenges open after your first prestige, and an active challenge has an ABANDON button (no reward, the run carries on as a normal run)
- Save Backup mentions Play Games cloud save; Stats wording fixed
- New app icon: a vacuum tube, a transistor and a chip on navy
- Music and sound effects pause when you leave the app or lock the screen, and resume if sound is still on
- Settings has separate Music and Effects volumes, plus a mute for each. The Sound switch still turns both off
- Send feedback opens an email to the developer with the app version filled in
- Play Games achievements for your first business, first manager, the big eras, your first prestige, your first investor milestone and your first challenge. More > Settings > Achievements opens the Play Games list when you're signed in
- More is tidier: Settings is first, the token shop lives only on the Board tab, and Stats holds the numbers
- Stats lists lifetime totals, your best runs, and each finished run (earnings, length, era, investors, challenge)
- What's new shows once after an update, and again from Settings
- Buys give a light vibration and milestones and prestige a stronger one. Vibration is on by default and does nothing in a browser
- Reset progress asks twice and makes you type RESET. It warns that the next cloud save replaces the Play Games copy
- Optional notification when away time hits its cap. Off unless you turn it on
- Number format: Short, Scientific, or Full names, used everywhere money and counts are shown
- Text size (Normal, Large, Extra large) and a high-contrast switch. Large text still lets the money bar scroll away if it gets too tall
- Prestiging for less than double your investors asks you to confirm, and shows the income before and after
- Hold a buy button to keep buying, faster the longer you hold. Scrolling away cancels it
- The loading screen rotates short facts from eras you've reached. A new game gets general facts only
