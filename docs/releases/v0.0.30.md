## What's new
- New facility rooms: the PC Build Bench and Research Servers (Mine and Cluster still say Coming soon), with real-model part icons and a Monitor Display setting.
- HQ is the area menu: each plant has a GO button (and a tutorial step for it). Rigs is a garage of PCs you saved at the bench, with parts and stats.
- Radio Valves (c. 1920) sit between Punch Cards and Relay Computers. The era header shows the decade you've reached, and a short arrival card appears when a new era or chip generation lands.
- Notices when research finishes and when material bins fill while you're away.
- History events: early-era contracts, the 1947 moth, Moore's Law (newer eras from 1965 cost 15% less), and recosted mega-projects.
- Starter research machines run faster, so Quantum is reachable in a few days of nonstop play or about a week of casual play.
- Anniversary weeks: on a real launch anniversary, that era's row earns ×1.25 for a week.
- Product recalls: five famous hardware flaws as one-time events; riding one out halves that era's income for a while.
- The Hardware Museum is organized by decades and has a Records wing: compute power versus real records, and a Moore's Law chart.
- A run calendar on the Runs page tracks whether you're ahead of history.
- You can start two businesses at once with two fingers.
