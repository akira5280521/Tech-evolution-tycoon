## What's new
- Locked badges, upgrades, Museum cards and projects stay hidden until you discover their era
- Badge pictures fit inside their frames
- The news ticker scrolls at half the speed, and pauses while you hold it
- Landscape: era cards use the full width, the money panel stays in place while you scroll, and the Welcome Back COLLECT button is always on screen
- Long era names, buy buttons, upgrade tags and bonus numbers fit on small phones and with large text sizes
