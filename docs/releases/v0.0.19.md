## What's new
- This update installs over 0.0.18. The package is still tech.evolution.tycoon
- In landscape, the side tab bar keeps its width under the camera cutout, the empty strip beside the page is gone, and text no longer scrolls under the status bar
- Research is its own tab, labeled RESEARCH. Badges and the Museum moved into MORE
- Each machine uses its own materials. An empty bin says Out of that material and makes nothing. A low bin says Short of that material and how much of the row is still running
- World events only show up in the eras they belong to. The Dot-com Bubble and Y2K each happen once per game, even after you prestige or fork the timeline

## Coming soon
- New eras that fill the rest of the timeline, up to smartphones
- More events from real history: Moore's Law, the first computer bug, chip shortages
- A look and sound for each era
