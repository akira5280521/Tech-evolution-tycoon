## What's new
- This update installs over 0.0.17. The package is still tech.evolution.tycoon
- New era: Jacquard Loom, c. 1804, between the Paper Mill and Punch Cards. It uses card stock from the Paper Mill. If your save already reached Punch Cards, the loom is started for you
- Material rows make 1 each cycle until you buy that row's x3 upgrade, which triples the material too
- Own 10 of an era to open the next one. A locked row shows how close you are
- Tips explain a feature the first time it appears, and ? buttons explain words in plain language. How to Play is split into sections, including Prestige (starting over)
- Saves are checked more carefully when they load, so a bad code cannot unpack without limit
- The end of every page stays above the tab bar, including on phones with a navigation bar

## Coming soon
- New eras that fill the rest of the timeline, up to smartphones
- More events from real history: Moore's Law, the first computer bug, chip shortages
- A look and sound for each era
