# Privacy policy — Tech Evolution Tycoon

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Tech Evolution Tycoon (`tech.evolution.tycoon`) is an offline idle game. This policy describes the Android app.

## Data the app collects

The app does not collect personal data. It does not create an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not include advertising or analytics.

## Data that stays on your device

Your empire — cash, businesses, upgrades, and settings — is stored only in the app's local storage on the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes that save. The developer does not receive a copy.

## Network

Sideloaded copies may ask GitHub whether a newer release exists. That request is an anonymous GET to `https://api.github.com/repos/akira5280521/Tech-evolution-tycoon/releases/latest`. It does not include your save, your name, an account, or an advertising id. The developer does not receive the request. If the phone has no network, the check is skipped.

If you tap Update, the app downloads that release's APK with the system download manager and opens Android's package installer. The file stays in the app's private storage. The request still carries no save or account.

Copies installed from Google Play do not run this check and do not download an APK. Play delivers those updates.

The game itself does not send gameplay or diagnostics anywhere.

## Children

The game has no account, no chat, and no ads. It is not directed at collecting information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published with the app's store listing.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
