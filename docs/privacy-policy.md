# Privacy Policy: Tech Evolution Tycoon

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers the Tech Evolution Tycoon game for Android (package
`tech.evolution.tycoon`), whether you install it from Google Play or from the
game's GitHub releases. "We" and "the developer" mean the independent
developer who makes the game.

## In short

- The game has **no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no account of
  its own**.
- Your progress is saved **on your phone**. The developer never receives it.
- If you **choose** to sign in with **Google Play Games**, Google stores a cloud
  copy of your save under your Google account.
- Copies installed from **GitHub** check GitHub for updates. That check sends
  nothing about you.

## What the game stores on your phone

Your game progress (cash, eras, upgrades, badges, Museum cards) and your
settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They stay
there. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them.

If you use **Save Backup** (More > Settings), the game creates a text code of
your save. The code only goes where you put it, such as a note or a message
to yourself. The developer doesn't receive it.

## Google Play Games (optional)

On copies installed from Google Play you can sign in with Google Play Games in
More > Settings > Account. You don't have to: every part of the game works
without signing in.

If you sign in:

- The game shows your Play Games name.
- The game saves a copy of your progress to your Play Games cloud save, so you
  can continue on another phone. You can turn **Auto cloud save** off in the
  same panel.
- Google Play Games Services, a service run by Google, handles the sign-in and
  stores the cloud save under your Google account. Google also collects its
  own information when you use its services, as described in Google's Privacy
  Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- The developer does not receive your save, your name, your email address or
  any other information from your Google account.

To delete the cloud save, remove the game's data from your Google Play Games
profile or your Google Account settings. To stop using it, sign out of Play
Games or turn off Auto cloud save.

## Update checks (GitHub copies only)

Copies installed from the game's GitHub releases check whether a newer version
exists by asking GitHub (`api.github.com`) for the latest release. The request
contains no save, name, account or advertising ID, and the developer doesn't
receive it. GitHub handles it under its own privacy statement:
https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

If you tap **Update**, the game downloads the new version from GitHub and opens
Android's installer.

Copies installed from Google Play do not do any of this. Google Play delivers
their updates.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** used for the optional Play Games cloud save, and on GitHub
  copies for the update check and download.
- **Install other apps** (GitHub copies only): lets the game hand a downloaded
  update to Android's installer when you tap Update. The Google Play version
  does not have this permission.

The game does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or
files.

## Children

The game is made for a general audience. It has no chat, no ads, no purchases
and no account, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child
has shared personal information with the developer, contact us below and it
will be deleted.

## Data sharing and sale

The developer does not collect, sell, rent or share your personal
information.

## Security

Your save stays on your device unless you choose Play Games cloud save, which
Google protects as part of its own service. Connections the game makes to
Google and GitHub use encrypted HTTPS.
