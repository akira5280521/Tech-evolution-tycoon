## Why some mid-eras look much faster

The 1860s Transcontinental Cable ($5.00M) and National Mainframe ($100M) now finish early and each grant a permanent +10% income bonus (stacked, about +21%). Moore's Law then cuts buy costs of 1965-and-later eras by 15%. Together they let the active bot open Workstations, Laptops, and Dial-up Web on the first run, just before the first prestige at 6h 28m.

On the baseline, the first prestige at 7h 15m landed after Supercomputers (6h 20m) and reset the run, so Workstations only arrived at 19h 56m on the next climb. After D those three eras arrive at 5h 49m–6h 20m (−69% to −71% vs baseline), which is a first-run vs post-prestige difference, not a late-game speed-up. Quantum is essentially unchanged (15d 6h vs 15d 7h).

The same early bonuses pull casual mid-eras forward (Workstations 3d 6h → 1d 11h, Tablets 8d 18h → 5d 2h). Casual first prestige and the RGB/Quantum wall are the same: prestige still at 1d 11h, RGB still 13d 3h, Quantum still not reached in 21 days.
