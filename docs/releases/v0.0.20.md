## What's new
- This update installs over 0.0.19. The package is still tech.evolution.tycoon
- The tutorial for a new game explains the goal, then what a business is, before the first tap
- The game says business instead of machine
- An ERA counter at the top shows how far along the timeline you are
- Rotation follows the phone's auto-rotate setting
- The Investors panel stays hidden until you have earned $1B in the run, or after you prestige
- Managers keep earning at half speed while the app is closed or in the background, for up to 24 hours
- Closing the app no longer pays more than leaving it open when a business is short of materials
