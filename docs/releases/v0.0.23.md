## What's new
- Starter goals: a new player gets one short target at a time under the cash readout, from tapping Timber Camp through the first Museum card, each with a small cash reward. Hide them from the card or from Settings. Players who already passed a goal do not get the reward again.
- The opening tutorial is six steps, and the buy control is one button that cycles X1, X10, X25, X50, X75, X100, and MAX. The cash number is labeled CASH.
- New businesses fill the gaps in the timeline: Relay Computers, Mainframes, Minicomputers, Game Consoles, Dial-up Web, Cloud Datacenters, Smartphones, and AI Accelerators. Their pictures are marked ICON SOON until the art is ready. Microprocessors and Graphics Cards change name as you own more of them.
- Play Games achievements stay marked Coming soon until the real achievement ids ship.
