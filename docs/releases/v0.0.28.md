versionCode: 28

## What's new
- New PC build bench on RIGS. Build a real PC from 1977 to 2024 (board, CPU, memory, graphics, sound, power, case, drives, network, cooling) with real model numbers and specs. Parts only fit the way they really did: sockets and chipsets, memory types, card slots, power plugs, case sizes and cooler mounts. A slow part holds back a fast one, and the bench adds R&D.
- Every part opens in its real year and is researched on TECH first. Parts go out of production when their era ends.
- Six new eras fill the gaps in history: Supercomputers, Workstations, Laptops, Multimedia PCs, 3D Accelerators and Tablets. Older saves are offered a fresh start once; KEEP PLAYING carries on as before.
- Monitors, SLI and CrossFire, TV tuners, RAID and capture cards, cases with airflow, mini-ITX and SFX builds, and an overclock switch with real heat.
- Research servers: networks, storage arrays, ARM servers, Cray and other classic machines, and two TECH projects at once with a four-core CPU on the bench.
- The Hardware Museum (MORE > MUSEUM): every piece of hardware in the game, room by room, with keyboards and mice and the history cards.
- Eras now also need earnings this run from Integrated Circuits on, and more with more investors and forks. The late eras need a TECH project first, and opening an era the first time uses some material when you tap OPEN ERA.
- INDUSTRIAL BASE now starts each run with 10 of every business from Timber Camp through Vacuum Tubes. If you have it, you keep it.
- The income limit is a little lower: it starts at x42 and rises by 5 with each fork. Strong saves may earn somewhat less; you keep all your investors, research and forks.
- Materials have bins now: the strip shows "840 / 1,000" and FULL. Tap a material to buy bigger bins, refine it, sell it or swap it.
- Real shortages happen once each, in their years: 1988 memory chips, the 1999 Taiwan earthquake, the 2011 Thailand floods, the 2017 crypto rush and the 2020 chip shortage.
- Refining: ten silicon, copper or germanium make one of a refined grade. Late research and late RIGS parts need them.
- RIGS parts use a little material by type; the BUY button shows what's missing.
- Two new lab projects use materials (FAB TEST RUN and CLEAN ROOM), and a new MATERIALS badge group.
- Play time and run records are counted correctly now, so RUNS and your stats show how long each run really took.
- The Fortnight badge now needs a real 14-day streak.
- The Investors card no longer jumps around, and the full-width PRESTIGE button sits at its top.
- New R&D tab in the bottom bar for the Research Lab.
- New HQ button beside TECH at the top. RIGS lives inside it, and HQ stays open after a prestige; each plant says which era it needs this run.
- Research shows progress bars on TECH, on the top TECH button and in the R&D lab.
- The game screen is split into SUPPLY (materials and refiners) and TECH (everything else). A row short of a material shows a Needs button that jumps to the row that makes it, and a dot on SUPPLY warns when a material runs out, fills up or hits a shortage.
- NEXT MOVE (MORE > HOW TO PLAY) has a GO button that takes you straight to the row it means.
- The thin gray bars at the screen edges after launch are gone, and there's a new dark splash screen.
- Popups no longer stack: shortages and dilemmas wait for the prestige report, and close the material sheet.
