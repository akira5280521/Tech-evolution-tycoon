## What's new
- Museum room badges: finish a room in the Hardware Museum to earn its badge, and the room's button gets a star.
- Today in History in the Museum shows hardware that launched on today's date; tap it to open the exhibit.
- The PC bench's upgrade plans can now suggest SLI and CrossFire builds: a second matching graphics card with its bridge.
- TAP TO RUN only shows during the tutorial.
- R&D points now come only from research machines; start with a punch-card tabulator in HQ.
- Managers can be switched on or off.
- Cycle times under a second now show exactly (0.65s, not 0.7s).
- The CASH box shows cash and income; what this run earned shows on the locked next era (and in MORE > STATS).
- Welcome Back says which material ran short while you were away, and for how long.
- Each tip shows once: an unread tip stays up until GOT IT instead of coming back every time.
