## What's new
- HQ and navigation: department cards, a compact status bar, Boost and Progress tabs, and a short tutorial with a lightbulb hint when you are stuck.
- R&D is reorganized: TECH lives in R&D, with a research queue, and the Home PC bench is separate from the server room.
- Every room has a Status panel (power, compute and alerts). Server cards show real-model spec bars.
- New machines, rooms, bench PCs and businesses come online over a short staged wait. Field Engineers, bought with earned R&D points, halve install times.
- A milestone ladder on the Runs tab pays era tokens on the way to your first fork. A full-screen card marks each new era.
- Welcome Back is one summary card. Settings are grouped. Only prestige and selling stock ask for confirmation.
- Long-press a number for its exact value, 48px tap targets, optional tap sound and vibration, and empty-state hints.
- Name your company, colorblind-safe colors, and Away Shifts (earned with R&D points) that extend the away cap.
- OPEN ERA now has a short set-up bar. Beating your best on Ahead of History pays an era token.
- Rolling save backups, notification switches, room art fixes, and lower battery use when room animations are off-screen.
