## What's new
- Research servers now bank a limited amount of R&D while you're away: 1 hour of their output, plus 1 hour for each TB of disk, up to 8 hours. Playing live isn't limited, and a project you started keeps going while you're away. Disks now matter twice: for speed, and for how much the servers can store.
- Four parts that said "Coming soon" are now in the game: the Tensor B200 card, 128 GB DDR5 memory, the immersion tank and the 1600 W supply.
- New "Copy my stats" button under More → Stats. It copies your progress (play time, which eras you reached and when, prestiges, R&D) as plain text to paste into a message. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Send Feedback can include your stats and any recent errors, so a bug report says what actually went wrong. It's on by default, you can turn it off, and you see the whole message in your email app before it's sent.
- The Tech page's seven tabs now wrap onto two lines instead of running off the edge of small screens.
- Facility pictures are zoomed in so the hardware is easy to see.
- Behind the scenes: updated libraries, and every change to the game is now tested automatically before it ships.
