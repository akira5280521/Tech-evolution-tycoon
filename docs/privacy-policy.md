# Privacy policy — Tech Evolution Tycoon

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Tech Evolution Tycoon (`com.techevolutiontycoon.game`) is an offline idle game. This policy describes the Android app.

## Data the app collects

The app does not collect personal data. It does not create an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not include advertising or analytics.

## Data that stays on your device

Your empire — cash, businesses, upgrades, and settings — is stored only in the app's local storage on the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes that save. The developer does not receive a copy.

## Network

The Android app does not request network permission and does not send gameplay, device identifiers, or diagnostics to a server.

## Children

The game has no account, no chat, and no ads. It is not directed at collecting information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published with the app's store listing.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
