## What's new
- Facilities (RIGS tab): research servers open with Microprocessors and make R&D points. With AI Accelerators, the Bitbark mine and the training cluster add a small extra income. Fit graphics cards, memory, power and cooling; each plant says what is holding it back.
- Tech timeline (TECH, beside the buy button): research real chips, memory, storage, cooling, power and networking in the order they came out. A project opens once your company reaches its year and the project before it is done.
- One research currency, R&D points. Your eras and the research servers fill the same pool, and specialties, lab projects and the tech timeline all spend it. Any server points from the last build are added in automatically.
- Research is kept when you prestige, fork the timeline or start a challenge.
- While the game is minimized or closed, facilities and R&D points earn at half rate, like your businesses.
- The TECH and FACILITIES shortcuts and the RIGS tab stay locked (???) until you reach them.
- Better battery life: the game stops working in the background, and bars and animations pause when the screen is off.
- Music now starts after your first tap, and the away-time reminder asks for permission and can open the phone's notification settings.
