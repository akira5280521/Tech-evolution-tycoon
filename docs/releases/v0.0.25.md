## What's new
- New pixel art: a new app icon, new pictures for all nine materials, and real icons for the Relay, Mainframe, Minicomputer, Game Console, Dial-up, Cloud, Smartphone and AI Accelerator eras.
- Facilities have art too: the Bitbark mine, training cluster, research servers and every part (graphics cards, memory, cooling, power supplies, racks and drives) now show a picture of the real hardware.
- A "?" on every item opens a short description of what it is and what it does. Locked items don't give anything away.
- R&D pacing: the research servers are now the main source of R&D points, and each extra rack costs more, so the plant grows a rack or two per era. Saves keep their balance.
- New tips when TECH, the research servers and the mine and cluster open, plus How to Play sections for R&D points, the Tech timeline and Facilities.
- The tutorial ends with a step that points at the locked TECH and RIGS buttons and says what they are.
