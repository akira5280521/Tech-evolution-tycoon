# Content rating — questionnaire notes

Complete Play Console → App content → Content rating (IARC). These notes match the game as shipped.

Expected result: **Everyone** / **PEGI 3** / equivalent. The questionnaire, not this file, issues the certificate.

## Violence

None. No combat, no blood, no weapons used against characters.

## Sexuality

None.

## Language

None. No profanity in the interface or in the scripted news lines.

## Controlled substances

None.

## Gambling

None. Investors, tokens, and prestige points are fictional and cannot be bought, sold, or cashed out. There is no real-money wager and no loot box.

## User interaction

- No chat
- No user profiles
- No sharing of personal information
- No user-generated content
- No location sharing

## Purchases and ads

- No in-app purchases
- No ads
- The app is free

## Miscellaneous Play declarations (not the IARC form)

- Ads declaration: **No, my app does not contain ads**
- News app: **No**
- COVID-19 contact tracing or status: **No**
- Government app: **No**
- Financial features: **No**
- Health: **No**
- Target audience: not primarily children. Do not opt into the Families program unless you intend to meet the Designed for Families rules. The game is suitable for a general audience.
- App access: all features are available without a sign-in. No test credentials are required.
